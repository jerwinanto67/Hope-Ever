Who we are

A decade of community-driven impact

Hope Ever Foundation is a registered non-profit established in 2012 under the Indian Trust Act, 1882. For over a decade we have worked in Tamil Nadu to empower Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, women and youth through sustainable livelihoods, education, child rights and environmental conservation — combining grassroots engagement with evidence-based planning and strong institutional partnerships.

Mission

To raise awareness and build community capacity for environmental conservation and economic self-reliance.

Vision

To ensure sustainable livelihoods for marginalized populations through eco-friendly practices such as organic agriculture and renewable energy.

Our journey

From one seed to fourteen districts

  1. 2012

    Rooted in Chennai

    Founded as a public charitable trust under the Indian Trust Act, 1882, to support vulnerable communities through sustainable development and inclusive growth.

  2. 14

    Districts reached

    From Chennai and Thiruvallur to Javvadhu Hills and Kanyakumari: livelihoods, child-rights clubs and women's groups across Tamil Nadu.

  3. 2020

    Skills for health

    Healthcare (GDA) training joins our skill programmes alongside tailoring, handicrafts, IT and entrepreneurship for rural youth and women.

  4. 15+

    Partners who believe

    Working with NITI Aayog, NABARD, NSDC, EDII, TAHDCO, ACA Denmark, CECOWOR and more to scale what works.

  5. Today

    Evidence-led, community-owned

    Recognised under Sections 12AA and 80G, with a team of professionals committed to lasting social and economic change.

What we do

Five branches, one root

From grassroots research to skill training and livelihood development, every program grows from the same commitment: communities at the centre of everything we do.

  • 01Community Outreach & AwarenessHealth, sanitation, education and tribal welfare campaigns that meet villages where they are.→
  • 02Livelihood Promotion & Rural DevelopmentMillets, organic farming, artisans and solar energy: income that respects the land.→
  • 03Skill Development & EmployabilityTailoring, healthcare, IT and entrepreneurship training for rural youth and women.→
  • 04Women & Community EmpowermentSelf-help groups, financial literacy and home-based livelihoods led by women.→
  • 05Research, Capacity Building & Participatory PlanningParticipatory appraisals and evidence-based planning so communities decide their own future.→
