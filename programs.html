What we do

Five thematic areas, one living system

From grassroots research and livelihood development to skill training, capacity building and evidence-based planning — driving sustainable change across Tamil Nadu.

01 / 05

Community Outreach & Awareness

Grassroots awareness campaigns and community-centred interventions focused on health, sanitation, education, tribal welfare, environmental sustainability and social inclusion. These initiatives strengthen public participation while addressing immediate local needs through one-day programs, workshops and village-level engagement.

  • Drug Awareness Programs
  • WASH & Hygiene Campaigns
  • COVID Relief & Welfare
  • Independence Day & Events
  • Educational Material Support
  • Tribal Welfare Programs
  • Child Protection Awareness
  • Public Health Awareness

Programs conducted

  • Drug awareness campaigns in rural communities
  • COVID-19 relief distribution drives in Thanjavur, Yercaud and Thiruvallur
  • WASH awareness initiatives with community participation
  • Educational scholarship support for women students in Ariyalur
  • Tribal welfare school book donation programs
  • Herbal sanitary napkin awareness in Chennai
  • Community celebration and welfare outreach programs
02 / 05

Livelihood Promotion & Rural Development

Supporting sustainable livelihoods through agriculture, millet promotion, artisan development, renewable energy awareness and rural entrepreneurship. These interventions improve income opportunities while preserving traditional knowledge and eco-friendly practices.

  • Millet Promotion Programs
  • Organic Farming Awareness
  • Value-Added Food Training
  • Rural Entrepreneurship
  • Tribal Handicraft Promotion
  • Renewable Energy Awareness
  • Solar Livelihood Initiatives
  • Artisan Development

Programs conducted

  • Millet cultivation and processing workshops in Chengalpattu and rural clusters
  • Value-added food production training programs
  • Solar lantern distribution and renewable energy awareness drives
  • Tribal handicraft awareness and artisan engagement activities
  • Rural entrepreneurship and micro-enterprise support programs
  • Community-based agriculture and sustainability awareness initiatives
03 / 05

Skill Development & Employability

Short-term and community-based skill initiatives that improve employability, entrepreneurship and self-reliance among youth and women — combining vocational exposure, technical orientation and livelihood-focused training across rural and semi-urban regions.

  • Tailoring & Embroidery
  • Handicraft Skill Exposure
  • Healthcare Orientation
  • IT & Digital Literacy
  • Entrepreneurship Awareness
  • Retail & Employability Skills
  • Soft Skills & Career Guidance
  • Youth Capacity Building

Programs conducted

  • PMKVY-linked community skill training activities
  • Hand embroidery and handicraft exposure programs with NIFT collaboration
  • Robotics and innovation awareness sessions at educational institutions
  • Youth employability and entrepreneurship workshops
  • Tailoring, mehendi and beautician livelihood training
  • Career guidance and workplace readiness sessions for rural youth
04 / 05

Women & Community Empowerment

Women-centred initiatives that strengthen self-help groups, improve financial awareness, encourage leadership participation and promote home-based livelihood opportunities for marginalized women and vulnerable communities.

  • SHG Strengthening
  • Financial Literacy
  • Home-Based Livelihoods
  • Women Entrepreneurship
  • Leadership Development
  • Community Participation
  • Livelihood Awareness Programs
  • Welfare Support Activities

Programs conducted

  • Agarbatti packing and home-based livelihood support activities
  • SHG capacity-building and enterprise awareness sessions
  • Women entrepreneurship and income-generation workshops
  • Community participation programs for women and vulnerable groups
  • Livelihood support initiatives for widowed and elderly women
05 / 05

Research, Capacity Building & Participatory Planning

Participatory assessments, grassroots consultations, institutional engagement and evidence-based planning that strengthen local governance, improve development strategies and encourage community-led decision-making.

  • Participatory Rural Appraisals
  • Community Consultations
  • Baseline Assessments
  • Village-Level Planning
  • Institutional Capacity Building
  • Stakeholder Workshops
  • Monitoring & Documentation
  • Knowledge Sharing Platforms

Programs conducted

  • Community consultations in tribal and rural regions
  • Village-level stakeholder and awareness workshops
  • Institutional coordination and participatory planning sessions
  • Capacity-building activities for local groups and volunteers
  • Monitoring, documentation and impact-learning initiatives
