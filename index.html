Who we are

A decade of community-driven impact

Hope Ever Foundation is a registered non-profit established in 2012 under the Indian Trust Act, 1882. For over a decade we have worked in Tamil Nadu to empower Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, women and youth through sustainable livelihoods, education, child rights and environmental conservation — combining grassroots engagement with evidence-based planning and strong institutional partnerships.

Mission

To raise awareness and build community capacity for environmental conservation and economic self-reliance.

Vision

To ensure sustainable livelihoods for marginalized populations through eco-friendly practices such as organic agriculture and renewable energy.
